    <p> HacKRNspace is a small non-profit organisation of which I am a board member. To quote the hackrnspace: "Hackrnspace is an association with the purpose of improving knowledge about technologies (such as computers, the internet, cybersecurity, etc) and giving the public the ability to use and interact with modern technology". For HacKRNspace I have made a website and several peices of promotional materials, including posters, flyers and stickers. </p>
    <img class="caseStudyImg"  src="/CaseStudies/hackrnspaceflyerfrontenglish.svg" />
    <img class="caseStudyImg" src="/CaseStudies/hackrnspaceflyerbackenglish.svg" />
    <p> This is a flyer set I made to explain what HacKRNspace is when we are presenting ourselves to the public, it is an a5 doublesided flyer. It is bright, happy and eyecatching. The idea is that we are welcoming and fun rather than the serious idea you would expect from a computer based group. I also chose a superhero type theme as we are a bunch of nerds, and therefore would appeal to other nerds. </p>
    
  <img class="caseStudyImg" src="/CaseStudies/postera3design2Swedish.svg" />
  <img class="caseStudyImg" src="/CaseStudies/postera3Swedish.svg" />
  <p> This poster set was for an event that we hosted where we were introducing the public to Linux as an alternative to windows 10 as it was being depreciated. The blank space is so we can write the date in when we have multiple events. The two themes are designed to attract multiple types of people. The colourful one is designed to attract younger groups of people - especially women whereas the blue is designed to pull in older people or those who are more used to the colours of windows. </p>
